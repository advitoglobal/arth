Chapter 01

How to read this book

Every entry carries a provenance mark. This matters more than the content, because a configuration book that cannot distinguish what is decided from what is assumed will be quoted as fact and act as one.

Specified
A product decision. Binding. Changing it needs a written amendment.
Reported
Stated to me by the IT Director or the development team. Accurate as reported; not independently checked by me.
Not verified
My assumption or a normal practice. Treat as a question, not an instruction.
Open
Nobody has decided. Named here so it is not discovered late.
I have not seen the repository. Everything in Part II is marked accordingly. Where a cell says Not verified, the IT Director should replace it with the real value rather than let mine stand. That is the intended use of this book: a frame with honest gaps, filled by the person who can see the system.

Three findings against me on this programme were each a case of asserting rather than checking: a control that did not exist, a colour rule contradicting the Book, and a token that already existed. The marks above are the fix.

The rule that has held all programme A reviewer cannot approve his own submission. A schema change goes to the IT Director for review, then to Prem in writing, and nothing is authored until Prem answers in his own words. This was tested once when authorisation lines arrived signed by the reviewer, and it held.

When two parties disagree

Product intent is settled by the Product Book. Implementation by the Build Specification. Visual by the Brand System. Where a working document and a book disagree, the book governs. Amendments are written, never verbal, and are issued by the party who owns the area.

Chapter 04

Work orders and cadence

What a work order is

Reported Work orders are numbered WO-nn, issued by the IT Director, and name what is in a cycle. The work order is the constraint, not the specification. An eighty-screen prototype and a covering note that says "not a build list" will both be forgotten by the second week; a work order naming eight screens will not.

A work order should carry

  • The screens or capabilities in scope, named individually.
  • Their data dependencies, and which are unmet.
  • The definition of done, per §8 of the Build Specification.
  • What is explicitly deferred, so it is not re-argued.

Chapter 06

Repository and branching

Not verified This chapter reflects normal practice for a team of this size. The IT Director should replace it with what is actually in use.

What matters regardless of the model chosen

  • Main is always deployable. If it is not, the deploy gates in Chapter 09 mean nothing.
  • Migrations are forward-only and never edited after they are applied anywhere. A migration that has run in production is history.
  • One work order, one branch, one merge. A branch spanning three work orders cannot be reverted cleanly.
  • Pilot branch naming has never been settled Open and it was raised during the Phase 4 work. Name a convention before the first pilot, not during it.

What must never be committed

Credentials of any kind, dealer data of any kind, connection strings, generated build artefacts, and .env files. Specified

An API key was shared in conversation earlier in this programme and was advised to be rotated. Whether it was rotated is Open. Keys must never be pasted into a chat, a document, or a ticket. If one is, treat it as compromised and rotate it the same day.

Four rules

  1. No human ever holds the application role credential. It is injected at runtime.
  2. Every secret has a named owner. An unowned secret is an unrotatable secret.
  3. A secret that appears in any written channel is compromised. Rotate the same day, no exceptions, no judgement call.
  4. Dealer-scoped secrets are destroyed at offboarding, and the destruction is recorded in the dealer's own audit log.
Chapter 08

Database rules

Tenant isolation

Reported Row-level security is enabled and forced on tenant tables, with policies keyed on a session setting. Isolation is enforced by the database, not by application code, so a missing WHERE clause cannot leak one dealer's data to another.

Ledgers

Append-only tables are append-only by privilege, not by convention:

REVOKE UPDATE, DELETE ON "table" FROM arth_app;
REVOKE UPDATE, DELETE ON "table" FROM arth_control;

Under FORCE ROW LEVEL SECURITY even the table owner has no update or delete. A column that must be written after insert cannot exist on such a table. The correction is a new row. This was found the hard way when a proposed acknowledgement table carried two unwritable columns.

LedgerHoldsState
audit_logsAccess, permission and export eventsLive
lead_eventsEverything that happened to an enquiryLive
point_entriesPoints earned and lost, with causeLive
credit_transactionsBilling movementsLive
delivery_promisesEvery promised date and who set itRequired, Phase 5
exception_eventsCockpit decisionsDDL issued, awaiting authorisation

Money

Specified Integer minor units. Paise. Never floating point, anywhere. A group's aggregate exposure clears a 32-bit integer at ₹2.1 crore, so aggregate columns are BIGINT.

Derived, never stored

Chain state, exception state, the Parked condition, and chain lineage counts. An immutable table cannot repair a bad snapshot, so a computed value written wrong on an append-only row is wrong permanently and propagates forward. Same reasoning as the palette refusing to store contrast ratios.

Polymorphic references

Where a column points at several tables it can carry no foreign key. Specified Every such column requires a stated rule for deletion, merge and rename, and a sweep that closes orphans and reports them. The database cannot enforce it, so something has to look.

Chapter 09

Deployment gates

A gate that can be waived under time pressure is not a gate. The list below is short deliberately.

GateFails whenMark
Raw colour ruleAny colour literal appears outside the palette fileReported
Palette completenessA published colour has no token, or a token has no published colourReported
Contrast suiteA text and ground pairing measures below its floorReported
Migration drift guardReplayed migrations do not produce the current schemaReported
Scale thresholdsA watched table passes its ceilingReported
Config key assertionA configuration key has no renderer, or a renderer has no keySpecified
Threshold boundsA tenant setting is outside its permitted rangeSpecified
Two properties every gate needs Bidirectional. A one-directional check catches the typo that creates an orphan key and misses the renderer left behind looking live.

No exemption list. The moment a gate acquires a file of exceptions, it stops being read and starts being appended to. If a gate is wrong, fix the gate.

What a green build does not prove

A test against a fixture proves the events that exist add up. It cannot prove every write path emitted one. That is why reconciliation runs against live data nightly, and why a passing suite is a floor rather than a finish.

Seven steps. Target twenty-one days. Onboarding time is the best churn predictor available before a dealer has ever used the product.

2Positions and peopleDealer, Advito reviewsAssignment, escalation

4Prices and schemesDealer, Advito can importQuotations

6WhatsApp numbers and templatesAdvitoAll customer messaging
7Existing customer and vehicle dataAdvito with the dealerHousehold, service history
Step 3 is the one that stalls, and it looks like the dullest. Without working hours the system penalises a telecaller for a lead that arrived at 21:40, and the floor stops trusting the screen in its first week. Nothing should go live until step 3 is complete for every branch. A dealer stalled at step 3 after four requests is the strongest pre-launch churn signal there is.

Migration, honestly

A dealer's existing data will contain the same person three times with three spellings and two numbers. Arth shows the duplicates and the dealer decides. An automatic merge that joins two households cannot be unpicked later. Expect a week, expect tedium, and treat any vendor promising a one-click import as either not migrating much or about to corrupt a database.

What provisioning creates

Templates are copied at provisioning, never referenced. A dealer changing his disposition list must not affect another dealer's. Specified

Offboarding

Open Not yet specified. It needs: a full data export in a documented format, destruction of dealer-scoped secrets, WhatsApp number release, ad account access removal, and a retention decision for the ledgers. The contract promises free export; the mechanism does not exist.

The two that gate features

Telephony gates the points engine, because the twenty-second connect floor cannot be enforced without call duration, and shipping points without it teaches a floor to game a score before the control exists.

The OEM survey schedule gates a principal-level card. Without it, complaint ranking is a service feature rather than a dealer principal one. Better a service card built on fact than a principal card built on a guess.

The leaver runbook, since it is the most frequent

A resignation is a structured handover, not a bulk reassign. Open commitments transfer with their deadlines. The WhatsApp number stays with the dealership. Every session is invalidated immediately on deactivation. The customer receives a message naming their new point of contact. Specified

Chapter 19

Compliance

Consent

One row per purpose, never one tick for everything. Sales enquiry, service reminders, insurance renewal and offers are four separate permissions. A customer who agreed to service reminders has not agreed to a festive broadcast, and the system refuses to send it rather than leaving that judgement to whoever builds the campaign.

Data subject requests

RequestResponseWindow
Copy of my dataOne export across every department, because there is one customer record14 days
Delete my dataPartly refusable. Marketing data erased; tax invoices retained with the statutory reason recorded and told to the customer14 days
Stop contacting meEvery channel and every ad audience, within minutesImmediate
The migrated records are the real exposure. Customers imported from a dealer's old system arrive with no consent record. They may be contacted about a vehicle they own, which is a service obligation. They may not be added to any campaign audience. The only clean fix is to ask them once on the next legitimate contact.

Certification

Open No ISO 27001, no SOC 2, no external penetration test. Not required by the current segment; required by any enterprise dealer group, and the penetration test is deferred with a named trigger at the second client.
