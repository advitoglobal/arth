## §2 THE ONE ADDITION — BOTH INSTRUMENTS READ SILENCE AS HEALTH

Deferral share measures **how** he exits. Events per identity measures **how much** is happening. **Neither can see a principal who does not exit at all.**

He opens the screen. Nine cards. He reads them, learns something, closes the tab, telephones nobody and records nothing.

- Deferral share: **undefined, or zero.** No deferrals.
- Events per identity: **low.** Few events.

**Both read as healthy. The screen is being completely ignored.** And this is not the unlikely case — it is the most likely failure of the whole design, because reading a screen and doing nothing is easier than any of the three exits and leaves no trace.

**A third measure, and it costs nothing because it needs no new data:**

> **The age of currently-open cards.** Any card whose identity has existed for more than N days with no exit event of any kind against it.

A principal using the screen produces exits. One ignoring it produces cards that simply get older. **Zero events is not the same as nothing wrong**, and the difference is visible in the age of what is sitting there.

Threshold from the pilot, per your own rule. **But the measure itself should be in the instrument from the first day**, because it is the one that catches the failure the other two mistake for success.
